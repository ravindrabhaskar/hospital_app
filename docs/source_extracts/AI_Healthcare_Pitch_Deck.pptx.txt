The problem
Healthcare is fragmented at the exact moment people need clarity.
People don't just need a doctor.
They need help deciding what to do next.
• Symptoms are difficult to interpret without context.
• Medical records are scattered across hospitals, PDFs and apps.
• Elderly and mobility-limited patients may struggle to access routine care.
• Families coordinate doctors, medicines, reports and emergencies manually.
• Teleconsultation alone does not solve physical examination or follow-up.
• Language and health-literacy barriers make digital healthcare harder to use.


The gap
Symptom → uncertainty → delay → fragmented care → missed follow-up
